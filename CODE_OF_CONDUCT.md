# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

IntentFrame is a security and systems project. We want it to be a serious, respectful space for people doing real work — developers, researchers, security practitioners, and builders.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Personal attacks, harassment, and bad-faith behaviour are not.

## Expected behaviour

- Engage with ideas, not with personalities.
- Give honest, direct feedback. Disagree openly and specifically.
- Assume good faith by default, especially for newcomers.
- Keep security-related discussion responsible — follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerability disclosure.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks in any form.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.
- Bad-faith behaviour designed to derail rather than contribute.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other communication connected to this project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening a private issue or contacting the maintainers via the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed and responded to. Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who violate this standard.

## Reference

This code of conduct is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
